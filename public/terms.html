What Kindway is

Kindway is a personal journal with optional AI reflections. It is a place to write and think. It is not a therapy or crisis service, it is not medical advice, and it cannot promise the confidentiality of a licensed professional. Replies written by AI can be wrong.

If you need help now

If you are in danger or thinking of harming yourself, please contact a person, not the app. In the app, open Extra support for Philippine hotlines and emergency numbers. In an emergency, call 911.

Your writing is yours

You own what you write. Kindway uses it only to run the app for you, as described in the privacy policy. You can export it, and you can delete it.

Using the app

  • You are responsible for your account and for keeping your password to yourself.
  • Use Kindway for your own journaling. Do not try to break it, overload it, or get into anyone else’s data.
  • You can stop at any time and delete your account from Settings.

As is

The beta is provided as it is, with no promise that it will always work, be available, or keep your data safe. Keep your own copy of anything you cannot afford to lose; the export in Privacy & data helps with that. To the extent the law allows, Kindway is not liable for losses from using the beta.
